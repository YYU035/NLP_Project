Corpus document 34498325: A conserved modified wobble nucleoside (mcm5s2U) in lysyl-tRNA is required for viability in yeast.

Paper verdict: INSUFFICIENT → INSUFFICIENT.

Original abstract; yellow marks benchmark rationale sentences
  1. Transfer RNAs specific for Gln, Lys, and Glu from all organisms (except Mycoplasma) and organelles have a 2-thiouridine derivative (xm(5)s(2)U) as wobble nucleoside.
  2. These tRNAs read the A- and G-ending codons in the split codon boxes His/Gln, Asn/Lys, and Asp/Glu.
  3. In eukaryotic cytoplasmic tRNAs the conserved constituent (xm(5)-) in position 5 of uridine is 5-methoxycarbonylmethyl (mcm(5)).
  4. A protein (Tuc1p) from yeast resembling the bacterial protein TtcA, which is required for the synthesis of 2-thiocytidine in position 32 of the tRNA, was shown instead to be required for the synthesis of 2-thiouridine in the wobble position (position 34).
  5. Apparently, an ancient member of the TtcA family has evolved to thiolate U34 in tRNAs of organisms from the domains Eukarya and Archaea.
  6. Deletion of the TUC1 gene together with a deletion of the ELP3 gene, which results in the lack of the mcm(5) side chain, removes all modifications from the wobble uridine derivatives of the cytoplasmic tRNAs specific for Gln, Lys, and Glu, and is lethal to the cell.
  7. Since excess of the unmodified form of these three tRNAs rescued the double mutant elp3 tuc1, the primary function of mcm(5)s(2)U34 seems to be to improve the efficiency to read the cognate codons rather than to prevent mis-sense errors.
  8. Surprisingly, overexpression of the mcm(5)s(2)U-lacking tRNA(Lys) alone was sufficient to restore viability of the double mutant.

Corpus document 2991954: Cdk11 is a RanGTP-dependent microtubule stabilization factor that regulates spindle assembly rate

Paper verdict: INSUFFICIENT → INSUFFICIENT.

Original abstract; yellow marks benchmark rationale sentences
  1. Production of Ran-guanosine triphosphate (GTP) around chromosomes induces local nucleation and plus end stabilization of microtubules (MTs).
  2. The nuclear protein TPX2 is required for RanGTP-dependent MT nucleation.
  3. To find the MT stabilizer, we affinity purify nuclear localization signal (NLS)-containing proteins from Xenopus laevis egg extracts.
  4. This NLS protein fraction contains the MT stabilization activity.
  5. After further purification, we used mass spectrometry to identify proteins in active fractions, including cyclin-dependent kinase 11 (Cdk11).
  6. Cdk11 localizes on spindle poles and MTs in Xenopus culture cells and egg extracts.
  7. Recombinant Cdk11 demonstrates RanGTP-dependent MT stabilization activity, whereas a kinase-dead mutant does not.
  8. Inactivation of Cdk11 in egg extracts blocks RanGTP-dependent MT stabilization and dramatically decreases the spindle assembly rate.
  9. Simultaneous depletion of TPX2 completely inhibits centrosome-dependent spindle assembly.
  10. Our results indicate that Cdk11 is responsible for RanGTP-dependent MT stabilization around chromosomes and that this local stabilization is essential for normal rates of spindle assembly and spindle function.

  2. The enzyme was purified 8000-fold to a specific activity of 25.6 mumol/min/mg.
  3. The subunit molecular mass is 53 kDa, and the native molecular mass is 210 kDa, suggesting a tetrameric structure.
  4. Soluble high Km 5'-nucleotidase is most active with IMP and GMP and their deoxy derivatives.

  6. The enzyme has a virtually absolute requirement for magnesium ions and is regulated by them.
  7. Purine nucleoside 5'-triphosphates strongly activate the enzyme with the potency order dATP greater than ATP greater than GTP.
  8. 2,3-Diphosphoglycerate activates the enzyme as potently as ATP.

  13. Inorganic phosphate shifts the hyperbolic substrate velocity relationship for IMP to a sigmoidal one.

  6. This functional integration of two RNA molecular patterns may provide a means to specifically sense and counteract replicating viruses.
